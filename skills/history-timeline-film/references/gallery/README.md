# Gallery: what the levels of quality look like

| file | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `reference-gallery-ride.jpg`, `reference-gallery-ride-2.jpg` | the finished reference film 《乘風騎行》 (high effort, 1,000–3,000 lines per scene): this is the bar for "museum quality" |
| `scene-looks-skeletons.jpg` | the 8 scene looks straight out of `new_film.mjs` (engraving, poster, blueprint, newsreel / screen, pixel, ink, fresco), placeholder tower still in place. Every scaffolded chapter starts here |
| `trial-easel-medium.jpg` | a medium-effort trial (easel + person, ~150–250 lines per scene): the medium reads, the subjects are simple geometry |
| `trial-gallery-ride-low.jpg` | a low-effort trial (gallery-ride + person, ~60–75 lines per scene, before the looks existed): flat, clip-art-like, empty band under the road |
| `demo-easel-keyframes.jpg` | the 3-chapter demo on the easel stage: intro, chapter changes, outro with the pinned paintings |

Use them to calibrate: a low-effort scene should at least look like the skeleton with a real,
recognisable subject; spend extra effort on detail, a foreground layer and a second motion.
